# Design: Programación AFRO IN 2027 (D9, first delivery)

## Problem

The next edition (AFRO IN 2027) must be published as soon as possible with a refreshed look, on desktop and mobile, in es/en/fr. Today `/programacion` shows the 2026 tour (collection `tour2026`, templates `ProgramacionIndexTemplate` / `ProgramacionSlugTemplate`). The 2026 edition must stay reachable as a past edition with the design it had.

## Chosen approach

"Festival zone" (Julio, 2026-10-27). The programación pages take the look of the 2027 posters, while the site's header, footer, brand red and body font stay the same so it still reads as afroin.org. The rest of the site (store, product, checkout, tickets, home) is decided in later deliveries.

Approved mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/CwcZwDuAuNv8pqPsMAysZJ (listing + Santa Marta, desktop and mobile).

Alternatives rejected: whole site dark now (too much scope before the edition is published; checkout readability), light page with a dark hero only (too far from the posters).

## Design

### Visual tokens (scoped to the festival pages)

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| night | `#07120D` | page background |
| jungle | `#123326` / `#1A4A35` | radial glow behind the hero |
| brand red | `#e31c25` (existing `--color-primary`) | "AFRO IN", day hearts, primary CTA |
| gold | `#F2C14E` | year, dates, times, frames, links |
| cream | `#F4EAD5` | text on dark |
| muted | `#C9BFA8` | secondary text on dark |
| heliconia | `#E8742A` | activity kind label |
| teal | `#47C1B0` | alternate day heart |

- Type: **Anton** (Google Fonts, display: titles, dates, times) + **Outfit** (existing, body). Anton is loaded only on these pages, through the same Google Fonts mechanism the site already uses (no npm dependency).
- Signature element: the posters' thin gold double frame, used on the stops block (listing) and on each day card (stop page). Hearts mark days, red and teal alternating.
- Header and footer: the site's existing `Header.astro` / `Footer.astro`, unchanged in look.

### Pages and URLs (rule 5: es/en/fr)

| Page | es | en | fr |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 listing (new design) | `/programacion` | `/en/program` | `/fr/programme` |
| 2027 stop (new design) | `/programacion/2027/<slug>` | `/en/program/2027/<slug>` | `/fr/programme/2027/<slug>` |
| 2026 listing (old design) | `/programacion/2026` | `/en/program/2026` | `/fr/programme/2026` |
| 2026 stop (old design) | `/programacion/2026/<slug>` | `/en/program/2026/<slug>` | `/fr/programme/2026/<slug>` |

- Old 2026 URLs (`/programacion/santa-marta`, `/programacion/palenque` and their en/fr versions) redirect 301 to the `/2026/` ones (`public/_redirects`, served by Workers static assets).
- `/programacion/2027` (no slug) redirects to `/programacion`.
- Navigation dropdowns that link to Santa Marta / Palenque point to the 2027 pages.

### Listing `/programacion`

1. Hero: one line of context, then "AFRO IN" (red) "2027" (gold) in Anton.
2. Gold double frame with the two stops: dates, city, venues summary, link. A stop without a published agenda shows "Programación muy pronto".
3. Guest artists grid (3 columns desktop, 2 mobile): photo, name, country, and date/venue or "fecha por anunciar".
4. "Edición 2026" band linking to `/programacion/2026`.

### Stop page `/programacion/2027/<slug>`

1. Hero: link back to the listing, dates (gold), city name (Anton, very large), one intro sentence, day jump links (Vie 15 · Sáb 16 · Dom 17).
2. One section per day: framed day card (heart, weekday, date, venue logo + name, area) and the activities list (time, artist photo, kind, title, artist and country, optional sign-up button). A day with no activities shows "Programación por anunciar".
3. Next stop band.
4. Schema.org `Event` JSON-LD per stop (as the 2026 pages have).
5. No "Entradas" band in this delivery (D2, later).

### Content model

- New collection `tour2027` (Markdown per stop and language, like `tour2026`): `name`, `startDate`, `endDate` (ISO), `location`, `order`, `intro`, `days[]` with `date` (ISO), `venue`, `venueArea`, `venueLogo?`, `mapUrl?`, `activities[]` with `time`, `title`, `kind?`, `artist?` (artist id), `signupUrl?`.
- Guest artists: one entry per artist with `name`, `country` (es/en/fr), `photo`, `order`, so a photo is replaced by swapping one file when AFRO IN sends clean PNGs.
- Weekday and date labels are computed from ISO dates per language, not typed.
- Images optimized and added to `src/assets/img/2027/` (artists from poster crops for now, venue logos reused from `src/assets/img/aliados/` where they exist, Co·KoA added).

## Edge cases & risks

- Rule 4: pages stay static (prerendered); no SSR image use.
- Redirects must return 301 on the Worker Preview (verify with curl, not only `astro dev`).
- The 2026 pages must look exactly as before (compare screenshots before/after).
- Activities at the same time on the same day (Choco and the battle at 4 pm) render in content order.
